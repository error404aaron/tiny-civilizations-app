    WELCOME, WORLDKEEPER

    History starts small.

    Three civilizations share a fertile island. They harvest food, raise new generations, and expand into neighboring territory. At crowded borders, stronger settlements may capture land.

    Your aim: help all three civilizations survive to year 200. Or simply watch what emerges. There is no civilization you must play.

    • Observe: tap land to inspect its food and owner.
    • Rain: restores food across a small area.
    • Forest: improves long-term food regeneration.
    • Meteor: scars one tile and reduces its owner's population.

    Influence regenerates over time. Winter lowers harvests. Select a civilization card to highlight its lands. Pause and speed controls let you set the pace.

    A simplified simulation, not a historical model. Progress lasts for this visit. Restart replays the seed; New world generates a fresh island.
